Amana Notes

Voice notes that never leave your phone. Press REC, talk, and the transcript writes itself, entirely on your device.

Download for Android

Android 7 or newer, 64-bit ARM. After installing, the app updates itself: it offers each new version and Android asks you to confirm.

Installing

  1. Download the APK on your phone and open it.
  2. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or Files app.
  3. Open Amana Notes, allow the microphone, and let it download what it needs once (about 1.3 GB, so use Wi-Fi).
