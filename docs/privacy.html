AMANA NOTES · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026

Amana Notes listens, transcribes, answers and finds your to-dos on your phone. Your recordings, transcripts and notes never leave your device unless you choose to share or back them up.

What the app stores

  • Audio of the recordings you make, and of the conversations Ears saves when you turn it on (compressed, on the phone).
  • Transcripts of that audio, and any edits you make to them.
  • To-dos and reminders Amana finds in your notes.
  • Your name, if you sign in (see below).
  • Settings, such as your chosen language and transcription mode.
  • Models for speech, Amana's voice and Amana's brain that you download.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps can't read it, and it isn't included in Android's automatic cloud backup.

What we collect

Nothing. Amana Notes has no online accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking. We don't run a server that receives your data, and we have no way to see your recordings, transcripts or notes.

Your name

Signing in stores only the name you type, on your phone, so Amana can greet you. It isn't an online account and is never sent anywhere. You can sign out in Settings; your notes stay.

Ears

When you turn Ears on, the app listens continuously and saves what it hears as notes on your phone, including other people's voices, until you turn it off. Android shows its microphone indicator and the app shows a notification the whole time. Saying "Amana, forget that" or tapping Forget drops the latest conversation.

Recording people can require their consent depending on where you are. Please tell the people around you when Ears is on, and turn it off where recording isn't appropriate.

Amana's brain

Answers, catch-ups, the to-dos Amana finds and whether one is done are worked out by an AI model that runs on your phone (Qwen 3 1.7B), and Amana's voice is generated on the phone too. Your notes are never sent to an online AI service. Like any AI, it can make mistakes; your recordings are always there to check.

When the app uses the internet

Recording, transcription, answers and reminders all work offline. The app connects to the internet only to:

  • Download models when you set the app up or choose one: speech, voice and speaker models from the open-source sherpa-onnx project on GitHub, and the brain model from Hugging Face. The large, optional accurate speech model downloads by itself, but only on Wi-Fi while the phone is charging.
  • Check for updates, at most once a day, and download a new version when you tap Update. Updates come from the Amana Notes releases on GitHub.

These requests are ordinary file downloads. They contain no recordings, transcripts, names or other personal information. Like any website, GitHub and Hugging Face receive your IP address and basic device information when you download from them; see GitHub's privacy statement and Hugging Face's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to listen while you record, while you ask Amana something, and the whole time Ears is on. Never otherwise.
  • Notifications: to show when the app is recording or listening, and to remind you of a to-do at the time you said.
  • Alarms & reminders: so those reminders arrive on time rather than up to an hour late.
  • Internet: for the downloads and update checks above.
  • Network state: to check for Wi-Fi, so the large optional speech model only downloads on Wi-Fi.
  • Install apps: to hand a downloaded update to Android's installer. Android asks you to confirm every install.
  • Foreground service, wake lock, audio settings and vibration: to keep long recordings and Ears running with the screen off, to set up the microphone, and for button feedback.

Sharing and backups

Text or audio leaves the app only when you copy or share it yourself, or back up your notes to a folder you choose; after that, the app or place you sent it to handles it under its own policy. Import backup reads only the folder you pick.

Deleting your data

Deleting a note removes its audio, transcript, to-dos and reminders from your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored, including your name and downloaded models.

Children

Amana Notes collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
