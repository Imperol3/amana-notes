AMANA NOTES · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 September 2026

Amana Notes records your voice and turns it into text on your phone. Your recordings and transcripts never leave your device unless you choose to share them.

What the app stores

  • Audio recordings you make by pressing REC.
  • Transcripts of those recordings, and any edits you make to them.
  • Settings, such as your chosen language and transcription mode.
  • Speech-recognition models you choose to download.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps can't read it, and it isn't included in Android's automatic cloud backup.

What we collect

Nothing. Amana Notes has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking. We don't run a server that receives your data, and we have no way to see your recordings or transcripts.

When the app uses the internet

Transcription works fully offline. The app connects to the internet only to:

  • Download speech models when you choose one. These files come from the open-source sherpa-onnx project on GitHub.
  • Check for updates, at most once a day, and download a new version when you tap Update. Updates come from the Amana Notes releases on GitHub.

These requests are ordinary file downloads. They contain no recordings, transcripts or personal information. Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address and basic device information when you download from it; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record after you press REC, until you stop. The app never listens at any other time.
  • Notifications: to show that a recording is in progress.
  • Internet: for the model downloads and update checks above.
  • Install apps: to hand a downloaded update to Android's installer. Android asks you to confirm every install.
  • Foreground service, wake lock and vibration: to keep long recordings running with the screen off, and for button feedback.

Sharing and backups

Text or audio leaves the app only when you copy or share it yourself; after that, the app you share it with handles it under its own policy. Import backup reads only the folder you pick.

Deleting your data

Deleting a note removes its audio and transcript from your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored, including downloaded models.

Children

Amana Notes collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
